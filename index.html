IB Physics SL Workbooks

Astrophysics

1 resource
  • Reading the Light of the Universe — IB Astrophysics Guided Research

    A week-long guided research project in which students pick a real star, pull genuine data from the SIMBAD and Gaia archives, and work through its thermal model, wave model, structure, evolution and scale before choosing an assessment option built around the star's fate and the Universe's future.

CSP

1 resource
  • Energy Balance & the Greenhouse Effect — Student Workbook

    A two-lesson workbook on radiation, albedo and Earth's energy balance, followed by the brief and support materials for the 10-hour Collaborative Sciences Project.

Currents and Circuits

1 resource
  • Current and Circuits — Student Workbook

    A complete, consolidated workbook covering the whole DC circuits topic in one place, from cells and charge through resistivity and combining resistors to emf, power and potentiometers.

Electromagnetism

3 resources
  • Electric and Magnetic Fields (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers electric charge, charging and discharging by induction, Coulomb's law, electric fields, the uniform field between parallel plates, and magnetic field lines.

  • Millikan's Oil-Drop Experiment — Student Workbook

    An interactive workbook and simulation recreating Millikan's oil-drop experiment to deduce the elementary charge and evaluate the experiment's assumptions and uncertainties.

  • Motion in Electromagnetic Fields — Student Workbook

    Covers the motion of charged particles in uniform and crossed electric and magnetic fields, the force on a current-carrying conductor, and the force between parallel wires.

Mechanics

6 resources
  • Kinematics — Student Workbook

    Covers position, velocity and acceleration, motion graphs, the SUVAT equations, vertical motion under gravity, and projectile motion including fluid resistance.

  • Forces — Student Workbook

    Covers forces as interactions, Newton's three laws, free-body diagrams, resultant force and equilibrium, contact forces, and field forces.

  • Conservation of Momentum — Student Workbook

    Covers momentum and its conservation, collisions, explosions, impulse, and Newton's second law for systems with changing mass.

  • Work, Energy and Power — Student Workbook

    Covers work and energy conservation, kinetic and gravitational potential energy, Hooke's law, Sankey diagrams, power, efficiency, and energy density.

  • Gravitation and Circular Motion (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers Newton's law of gravitation, gravitational field strength, circular motion, Kepler's laws of orbital motion, and orbital speed.

  • Simple Harmonic Motion — Student Workbook

    Covers the conditions for SHM, the mass–spring system and simple pendulum, energy changes during SHM, phase angle, and the SHM data-booklet equations.

Nuclear Physics

3 resources
  • The Nucleus & Atomic Spectra — Student Workbook

    Covers the Geiger–Marsden–Rutherford experiment, nuclear notation and isotopes, and the evidence for discrete energy levels from emission and absorption spectra.

  • Radioactivity & Half-Life — Student Workbook

    Covers radioactivity, the strong nuclear force, detecting radiation, alpha, beta and gamma decay, and randomness, activity and half-life.

  • Binding Energy & Nuclear Stability — Student Workbook

    Covers binding energy and mass defect, fission and chain reactions, and how a nuclear power plant manages its fission products.

Thermal Physics

3 resources
  • Methods of Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers particles in solids, liquids and gases, thermal equilibrium, internal energy and phase change, and the three ways heat transfers, including Wien's law.

  • Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers the heating curve, internal energy, specific heat capacity, and specific latent heat.

  • Modelling a Gas (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers pressure, the kinetic model of a gas, the ideal gas law, and the internal energy of an ideal gas.

Waves

5 resources
  • The Nature of Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers what a wave is, transverse and longitudinal waves, describing and calculating wave speed, sound and electromagnetic waves, and reflection at boundaries.

  • Velocity Change in Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers wave behaviour at boundaries, refraction, refractive index, Snell's law, critical angle and total internal reflection, and applications such as optical fibres.

  • Interference of Waves (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers superposition, coherence and interference patterns, path difference, and Young's double-slit experiment.

  • Standing Waves and Resonance — Student Workbook

    Covers the formation of standing waves, nodes and antinodes, standing wave patterns on strings and in pipes, resonance, and damping.

  • From a Passing Siren to an Expanding Universe — IB Guided Investigation

    A three-part guided investigation into the Doppler effect, red shift and the expanding Universe, using interactive simulations and real galaxy recession data.
