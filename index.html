IB Physics SL Workbooks

Astrophysics

1 resource
  • Reading the Light of the Universe — IB Astrophysics Guided Research

    A week-long guided research project in which each student picks a real star and pulls its own data from the SIMBAD and Gaia archives, then works through five stages — the thermal model (Wien's law, Stefan–Boltzmann, luminosity), the wave model (Doppler shift and Hubble's law), stellar equilibrium and fusion, evolution on the HR diagram, and distance/radius from parallax — before synthesising it all into an argument about the star's fate and the future of the Universe. Includes three assessment options: a written investigation, a comparative poster with oral defence, or a structured exam-style paper.

CSP

1 resource
  • Energy Balance & the Greenhouse Effect — Student Workbook

    A two-lesson workbook on radiation, emissivity, the solar constant and albedo, followed by Earth's energy balance and the greenhouse effect. Includes a synthesis task on real planetary energy budgets, an interactive inverse-square-law simulation, and the brief for the 10-hour Collaborative Sciences Project investigating albedo or foliage energy shielding, with an understanding checklist and self-marking answer key.

Currents and Circuits

1 resource
  • Current and Circuits — Student Workbook

    A complete, consolidated workbook covering the whole topic in one place: cells and circuit diagrams, charge and current, potential difference and Kirchhoff's laws, series and parallel circuits, conductors/insulators and the origin of resistance, resistance/Ohm's law and I–V characteristics, resistivity, combining resistors, emf and internal resistance, electrical power, and variable resistors and potentiometers, with a glossary.

Electromagnetism

3 resources
  • Electric and Magnetic Fields (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers electric charge, charging and discharging by induction, Coulomb's law (with an interactive inverse-square-law simulation), electric fields, the uniform field between parallel plates, and magnetic field lines, with an equation summary, mixed practice and a glossary.

  • Millikan's Oil-Drop Experiment — Student Workbook

    An interactive workbook and simulation recreating Millikan's oil-drop experiment: why the experiment mattered, the apparatus, balancing the forces on a charged drop, running the simulation to collect data, deducing the elementary charge, evaluating the experiment's assumptions and sources of uncertainty, and an exam-style question.

  • Motion in Electromagnetic Fields — Student Workbook

    Covers conventional current versus the flow of electrons, motion in uniform electric and uniform magnetic fields, a "predict the path" quiz, motion in crossed electric and magnetic fields, the force on a current-carrying conductor (F = BIL), and the force between parallel current-carrying wires, with an equation summary and glossary.

Mechanics

6 resources
  • Kinematics — Student Workbook

    Covers position, distance and displacement, speed and velocity, acceleration, motion graphs, the SUVAT equations of motion, vertical motion under gravity, projectile motion, and fluid resistance and projectiles, with a glossary.

  • Forces — Student Workbook

    Covers forces as interactions, Newton's three laws of motion, free-body diagrams, resultant force and equilibrium, contact forces (normal force, friction, drag and buoyancy), and field forces, with a glossary.

  • Conservation of Momentum — Student Workbook

    Covers momentum and its conservation, collisions in one dimension, elastic and inelastic collisions, explosions, impulse, and Newton's second law revisited for systems with changing mass, with a glossary.

  • Work, Energy and Power — Student Workbook

    Covers work done by a force, energy and its conservation, kinetic and gravitational potential energy, Hooke's law and elastic potential energy, conservation of mechanical energy, Sankey diagrams, power, efficiency, and energy density, with a glossary.

  • Gravitation and Circular Motion (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers Newton's law of gravitation, gravitational field strength and field lines, circular motion, Kepler's laws of orbital motion, and orbital speed, with a glossary.

  • Simple Harmonic Motion — Student Workbook

    Covers the conditions for simple harmonic motion, describing SHM, the time period of a mass–spring system, the simple pendulum, energy changes during SHM, phase angle, and solving problems with the SHM data-booklet equations.

Nuclear Physics

3 resources
  • The Nucleus & Atomic Spectra — Student Workbook

    Covers the nucleus and the Geiger–Marsden–Rutherford experiment, nuclear notation and isotopes, evidence for discrete energy levels from emission and absorption spectra, and photons and atomic transitions, with a glossary.

  • Radioactivity & Half-Life — Student Workbook

    Covers what radioactivity is, the strong nuclear force, detecting radiation and background radiation, alpha, beta and gamma decay, penetrating power and ionising ability, and randomness, activity and half-life, with a glossary.

  • Binding Energy & Nuclear Stability — Student Workbook

    Covers binding energy and mass defect, binding energy per nucleon, fission and chain reactions, how a nuclear power plant works, and the management of fission products.

Thermal Physics

3 resources
  • Methods of Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers particles in solids, liquids and gases, temperature and thermal equilibrium, internal energy and phase change, the three ways to transfer thermal energy, conduction, convection, radiation and black bodies, and Wien's law and the black-body spectrum, with a glossary.

  • Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers the heating curve, internal energy along the curve, specific heat capacity, specific latent heat, and putting it all together, with a glossary.

  • Modelling a Gas (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers pressure and amount of substance, the kinetic model of a gas, the ideal gas law, and the internal energy of an ideal gas, with a glossary.

Waves

5 resources
  • The Nature of Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers what a wave is, transverse versus longitudinal waves, describing a wave (wavelength, amplitude, period), wave speed (v = fλ = λ/T), sound and electromagnetic waves, mechanical vs. electromagnetic waves, wavefronts and rays, and reflection at boundaries, with a glossary.

  • Velocity Change in Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers wavefronts and rays, wave behaviour at boundaries (reflection, transmission and refraction), why refraction happens, wavefront-ray diagrams of refraction, refractive index, Snell's law, critical angle and total internal reflection, and applications such as optical fibres, with a glossary.

  • Interference of Waves (SL) — Student Workbook

    Covers the superposition of waves and pulses, coherence and interference patterns, path difference and the conditions for interference, and Young's double-slit experiment, with a glossary.

  • Standing Waves and Resonance — Student Workbook

    Covers the formation of standing waves, nodes and antinodes, standing wave patterns on strings and in pipes, natural frequency and resonance, and damping, with a glossary.

  • From a Passing Siren to an Expanding Universe — IB Guided Investigation

    A three-part guided investigation: the Doppler effect for sound and light with an interactive wavefront simulator, red shift and the comparison of spectra with a linked external redshift simulator, and an open investigation into the expanding Universe using an elastic-band model and real galaxy recession data, finishing with a written report or investigation journal.
